04  /  The engagement

Six weeks. Fixed price. Fixed end date.

Weeks 1 to 2  /  Find
Two of our engineers on your floor, with your people, on your data. We come back with the three problems worth building. You pick one.
Weeks 3 to 6  /  Build
We build it and put it into production. Your team works beside us throughout, so they can run it after we go.
  • You own everything. The code, the hardware, the documentation. No licence, no subscription, no lock-in.
  • If week two shows there is nothing worth building, we say so and we stop. You pay for two weeks and we part on good terms.
